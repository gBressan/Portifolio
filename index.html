about

Engineering that makes AI dependable.

I'm a software engineer working at the intersection of AI and backend engineering — part of an R&D squad that applies artificial intelligence to practical business problems and turns research into real-world products.

My work spans AI engineering, generative AI, LLMs, RAG, vectorization and semantic search. I build and integrate AI solutions with Java, Spring Boot, Python and FastAPI — using Azure OpenAI, LangGraph, LangChain, LlamaIndex and Langfuse to connect LLMs to enterprise data.

On the backend, I build REST APIs and microservices on the Spring ecosystem, backed by PostgreSQL, Oracle, Redis and Elasticsearch — with a focus on clean architecture, scalability, resilience, testing and maintainability.

What drives me is agentic AI and LLM applications — and the challenge of combining modern AI capabilities with reliable software engineering.

I'm also studying machine learning — going deeper into the models, data and training behind the AI systems I build.
